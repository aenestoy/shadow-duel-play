Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026 · This page is in English and Turkish; players who use another language see the English text.

In short: if you play online, Shadow Duel saves a nickname and your scores so they can appear on the public leaderboards. We don’t ask for your real name, email, age or location, the game has no advertising, analytics or tracking cookies of its own (any ads you see come from the site you play on), and we never sell your data.

1. Who we are

Shadow Duel is a browser game made by an independent developer. For anything about this policy or your data, email aenestoy@gmail.com.

2. What data we collect

On our leaderboard server

Only when the game is connected to our online leaderboards. As a guest, nothing about you is saved there until you choose a nickname.

  • Your nickname (3–16 characters, chosen by you) and a player number given by the server.
  • Scores: your best score per leaderboard, month and ninja, the fight time, the date, and a short match summary used to check the score is plausible (game version, game mode, fight length, fights and rounds won, hits, opponent and highest opponent level).
  • Dan rank (current and highest) and monthly titles: the top 10 of each finished month are kept in the Hall of Champions archive with the nickname, ninja, score and Dan at that time.
  • If you are signed in to CrazyGames: your CrazyGames user ID and username (shown as your name on the leaderboards). The game gets a signed token from CrazyGames and our server checks it; the token itself is not stored. Your profile picture is not stored.
  • Keys, stored only as one-way hashes: the random device key the game creates on your device, recovery codes (if you ask for one), and short-lived sign-in sessions for CrazyGames accounts (valid 12 hours).
  • Anti-cheat and rate-limit records: the times you submit scores (deleted after about 2 days), and, when a new player registers or someone enters a recovery code, a salted one-way hash of the IP address (for IPv6, of its first half) with the time and, for recovery attempts, whether the code was right (deleted after about 1 day). The IP address itself is not stored in our database.
  • A record of account events (linking a guest to a CrazyGames account, merging, recovery): player numbers and time only.

Your nickname, Dan, titles, scores, ninja, fight time and date are public: every player can see them on the leaderboards. Please don’t use your real name or any personal information as a nickname.

On your device

Your settings (sound, language, graphics, controls) and progress (journeys, honor, unlocked ninjas and arenas, combo trials, your local leaderboard, your nickname and the device key) are kept in your browser’s local storage. On CrazyGames and Yandex Games a copy of your progress is also kept in the portal’s own cloud save, so it can follow your account there.

What we don’t collect

No real names, email addresses, passwords, phone numbers, precise location, contacts, photos or payment details. The game sets no cookies of its own and contains no advertising or analytics code of its own.

3. Why we use it

  • To show the online leaderboards, the monthly tournament, Dan ranks and the Hall of Champions, and to award monthly titles and Champion colors.
  • To keep your titles, Dan and scores with your CrazyGames account (if you sign in), or to restore them on another device with your recovery code.
  • To keep the leaderboards fair: checking scores, blocking offensive nicknames, and limiting how often scores, new players and recovery attempts can be sent.

4. Where it is stored

Our leaderboard database runs on Supabase (our hosting provider) in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany). Like any web service, Supabase handles your IP address to deliver the game’s requests and may keep short technical logs under its own terms. When the game can’t reach the server, or on sites where it doesn’t connect to it at all (for example Poki), everything stays on your device.

5. How long we keep it

  • Nickname, player number, scores, Dan and titles: while the online leaderboards run, or until you ask us to delete them.
  • Score submission times: about 2 days. Hashed IP records: about 1 day. CrazyGames sign-in sessions: 12 hours.
  • Data on your device: until you clear your browser’s site data for the game (or the portal deletes its cloud save).

6. Who we share it with

Nobody. We don’t sell, rent or give your data to anyone, and we don’t use it for advertising. Supabase stores it for us as our hosting provider. The public leaderboards show what is listed above to other players.

The game sites where you play Shadow Duel (such as CrazyGames, Poki and Yandex Games) run their own accounts, ads, cookies and analytics. They process that data themselves, under their own privacy policies; please read those as well.

7. Children

Shadow Duel is meant for players aged 12 and over (PEGI 12). We collect no data beyond a nickname, scores and the technical records above, and we never ask for contact details. If you are a parent or guardian and want your child’s nickname and scores removed, email us and we will delete them.

8. Your rights

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the data we hold about you. Email aenestoy@gmail.com with your nickname and, so we find the right player, your recovery code or your CrazyGames username. We reply and, for deletion requests, delete the data within 30 days. Guest players can change their nickname in the game at any time. If you live in the EU, the EEA or the UK, you also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

9. Changes

If the game starts handling data differently, we will update this page and the date at the top.

2. Hangi verileri topluyoruz

Sıralama sunucumuzda

Yalnızca oyun çevrimiçi sıralamalarımıza bağlıyken. Misafir olarak oynarken sen bir takma ad seçene kadar orada seninle ilgili hiçbir şey kaydedilmez.

  • Takma adın (3–16 karakter, senin seçtiğin) ve sunucunun verdiği oyuncu numarası.
  • Skorlar: sıralama, ay ve ninja başına en iyi skorun, dövüş süresi, tarih ve skorun makul olduğunu denetlemek için kısa bir maç özeti (oyun sürümü, oyun modu, dövüş uzunluğu, kazanılan dövüş ve raundlar, isabetler, rakip ve en yüksek rakip seviyesi).
  • Dan rütben (şu anki ve en yüksek) ve aylık unvanlar: biten her ayın ilk 10'u, o anki takma ad, ninja, skor ve Dan ile Şampiyonlar Salonu arşivinde tutulur.
  • CrazyGames'e giriş yaptıysan: CrazyGames kullanıcı kimliğin ve kullanıcı adın (sıralamalarda adın olarak görünür). Oyun CrazyGames'ten imzalı bir belge alır, sunucumuz onu doğrular; belgenin kendisi saklanmaz. Profil resmin saklanmaz.
  • Yalnızca tek yönlü özet olarak saklanan anahtarlar: oyunun cihazında ürettiği rastgele cihaz anahtarı, (istersen) kurtarma kodları ve CrazyGames hesapları için kısa ömürlü oturumlar (12 saat geçerli).
  • Hile ve hız sınırı kayıtları: skor gönderdiğin zamanlar (yaklaşık 2 günde silinir); yeni bir oyuncu kaydolduğunda ya da biri kurtarma kodu girdiğinde IP adresinin tuzlanmış tek yönlü özeti (IPv6'da ilk yarısının) ve zamanı, kurtarma denemelerinde kodun doğru olup olmadığı (yaklaşık 1 günde silinir). IP adresinin kendisi veritabanımızda saklanmaz.
  • Hesap olaylarının kaydı (misafiri CrazyGames hesabına bağlama, birleştirme, kurtarma): yalnızca oyuncu numaraları ve zaman.

Takma adın, Dan'ın, unvanların, skorların, ninjan, dövüş süren ve tarih herkese açıktır: her oyuncu sıralamalarda görebilir. Lütfen takma ad olarak gerçek adını ya da kişisel bir bilgini kullanma.
